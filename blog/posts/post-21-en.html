Juso New Spot Field Report 2026

Minamo Juso
A Walking-Distance New Riverside Spot from Oideya [2026]

August 25, 2026Oideya
On August 8, 2026, a brand-new riverside spot called Minamo Juso grand-opened on the Yodogawa embankment in Juso — right next to Oideya. Twenty-two container-style food stalls now line the top of the levee, with the Umeda skyline visible across the river and the water right in front of you. It's a genuinely open, unexpected setting to find in the middle of the city, and it's only about 15 minutes from Oideya by train and on foot. This is a report on walking through Minamo Juso not long after it opened.
Grand opened August 8, 2026 Food stalls, BBQ, river cruises Walking distance from Oideya

What Minamo Juso actually is

Minamo Juso is a public-private redevelopment project run jointly by the Yodogawa Ward Office and a consortium of RETOWN, Rui Design Office, and OneOsaka River Cruise. It uses the national "Kawa-machi-zukuri" (river-town development) support programme to activate a stretch of the Yodogawa embankment near Juso that had previously seen little development.

The site is built around five components. The centrepiece is a food stall village with 22 shops connected to Osaka (expanding to as many as 29). Alongside it sit a reservation-based urban BBQ facility, a nature education programme built around the Yodogawa's ecology, weekend community events, and river cruises circling the Juso area. It's designed to be more than a strip of restaurants — it's meant to activate the embankment itself as a destination.

Getting there from Oideya — remarkably close

One of Minamo Juso's biggest draws for anyone staying at Oideya is how close it is. From Kanzakigawa Station, one stop on the Hankyu Kobe Line (about 3 minutes) puts you in Juso. From the east exit, a gentle slope leads up to the flat top of the embankment, where the row of container-style stalls comes into view. Reported walking times vary between 5 and 7 minutes depending on the source, but excluding traffic lights it feels closer to 5.

Effectively, there's now a new, easy option for "let's just walk over to Juso for dinner" during a stay at Oideya. Juso is already known as the birthplace of negiyaki, and now there's a second good reason to head that direction.

How to get there from Oideya

Kanzakigawa Station (Hankyu Kobe Line) → Juso Station (1 stop, ~3 min) → 5–7 min walk from the east exit. Total journey including the walk is around 15 minutes. Worth keeping in mind as a new go-to for dinner during your stay, or as a stop on the way back from a day of sightseeing.

Walking through — what it was actually like

The view from the top of the embankment

The food stall village sits directly on the flat crest of the Yodogawa embankment, which means the river's roughly 800m width is right there in full view. Looking across, the Umeda Sky Building and Grand Front Osaka's towers are clearly visible — it's a genuine surprise to find this much open sky in central Osaka. After dark, the lights of the opposite bank overlap with the glow of the stalls, giving the space a completely different character from daytime.

A distinctive look built from shipping containers

This isn't the loose, improvised "yatai" image most people picture. Every shop at Minamo Juso is a proper container-based unit, with real kitchen facilities capable of handling raw food and rice — a genuine restaurant standard rather than a stripped-down street stall. Seating is set up in front of each unit, positioned to face the river and the Umeda skyline. The uniform rows of containers create a distinctive, orderly look that's unlike anywhere else in the city.

What's on offer at the food stall village

The 22 shops (listed roughly west to east, starting near Juso Station) span a genuinely wide range of cuisines connected to Osaka.

Bar / izakaya Ramen Oden Sushi Yakitori Italian Café Takoyaki highball bar Japanese sweets & light meals

From classic Osaka staples like sushi, yakitori, and oden through to Italian and café options, the range is genuinely broad — and part of the appeal of a stall village is that you're not locked into one restaurant. Grazing across a few shops in one evening is the natural way to use the space. Seven more shops are expected to join, taking the total to 29, so repeat visits should keep turning up something new.

Beyond the food stalls

WONDER BBQ FIELD Juso

A reservation-based urban BBQ facility with river views. Set up for company events, clubs, and school outings as well as casual groups.

River cruises

Cruises circling the Juso area run mainly on weekends. Around 20 minutes, roughly ¥2,000 for adults, ¥1,000 for children (ages 6–12), free for younger kids (BBQ guests get a discount). Routes toward Hirakata and the Okawa river are planned on an irregular basis.

Nature education

Periodic environmental learning programmes built around the Yodogawa ecosystem — a good fit for families with kids.

Weekend events

Community-linked events run mainly on Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays, so the site takes on a different feel from one weekend to the next.

Practical notes before you go

Hours vary by shop

There's no single set of opening hours for the whole site — each stall sets its own. If you have a particular shop in mind, checking the official site (minamo-juso.com) beforehand is the safest bet.

When to visit

As a newly opened, currently talked-about spot, weekends and any period of press attention are likely to draw crowds. A weekday evening, ideally around sunset, tends to give you both the view and a more relaxed dining experience.

What to bring

Since this is an open-air riverside site, conditions shift noticeably with the season and weather. Summer days can be hot with no shade cover; evenings can turn surprisingly cool with the river breeze, so bringing a light layer is worth it.

Combining it with a stay at Oideya

Minamo Juso is a particularly good fit for anyone staying at Oideya — close enough to be a genuinely easy evening trip, whether as a closing move after a day of sightseeing or as dinner on your first night.

Sample plan

Late afternoon: return to Oideya via Umeda after a day of sightseeing → Freshen up: shower and change at Oideya → Evening: Hankyu from Kanzakigawa to Juso (1 stop, ~3 min), then walk to Minamo Juso → Food stall hopping: work through a few shops while watching the river turn to evening light → Return: walk and train back to Oideya (barely any travel time at all).

Combined with Juso's negiyaki or the local restaurants near Kanzakigawa, this gives Oideya guests yet another easy option for where to eat during a stay. There's also something worth catching about the freshness of a spot this new — if this report has piqued your interest, it's worth experiencing while it still has that just-opened energy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Minamo Juso?
Minamo Juso is a new mixed-use riverside spot that grand-opened on August 8, 2026, on the Yodogawa embankment in Yodogawa-ku, Osaka. It's built around a food stall village of 22 container-style shops (expanding to as many as 29), alongside a reservation-based urban BBQ facility called WONDER BBQ FIELD Juso, weekend river cruises, and periodic nature education programs.
How do I get to Minamo Juso? How far is it from Oideya?
It's about a 5–7 minute walk from Hankyu Juso Station. From the east exit, a gentle slope leads up to the embankment where the venue sits. From Oideya (Kanzakigawa Station), it's one stop on the Hankyu Kobe Line to Juso (about 3 minutes), meaning the full journey including the walk runs around 15 minutes.
What are Minamo Juso's opening hours?
There's no single set of hours for the whole site — each stall sets its own hours. If you have a specific shop in mind, checking the official website beforehand is the most reliable approach.
Can I do BBQ at Minamo Juso?
Yes. A reservation-based urban BBQ facility called WONDER BBQ FIELD Juso is attached to the site. Since it operates on a reservation system, you'll need to book in advance through the official website if you want to use it.
